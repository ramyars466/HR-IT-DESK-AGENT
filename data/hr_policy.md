## Section 1: Leave & Attendance Policy

### 1.1 Leave Entitlements
All full-time employees and extended interns are entitled to the following annual paid leaves, credited on a pro-rata basis:
- **Casual Leave (CL):** 12 days per calendar year. Intended for personal matters, emergencies, and short unplanned leaves (up to 3 consecutive days at a time).
- **Sick Leave (SL):** 10 days per calendar year. A medical certificate from a registered practitioner is required if sick leave extends beyond 2 consecutive business days.
- **Earned/Privilege Leave (EL):** 15 days per calendar year, accrued monthly after probation. Requires prior manager approval at least 7 business days in advance.
- **Public Holidays:** 12 declared public holidays per year (published on the internal company intranet at the start of each calendar year).
- **Maternity & Paternity Leave:** 26 weeks paid maternity leave; 2 weeks paid paternity leave in compliance with statutory norms.

### 1.2 Carry-Forward & Encashment Rules
- **Casual & Sick Leaves:** Unused Casual Leaves and Sick Leaves lapse automatically on December 31st of each calendar year and cannot be carried forward or en-cashed.
- **Earned Leaves:** Up to a maximum of 10 unused Earned Leaves can be carried forward to the following calendar year. Any unused balance exceeding 10 days at year-end will be automatically en-cashed at basic salary rates during January payroll.

### 1.3 Working Hours & Flexibility
- Standard working hours are **9:30 AM to 6:30 PM IST**, Monday through Friday.
- A flexible band of up to ±1.5 hours is permitted with manager alignment, provided core collaboration hours (**11:00 AM to 4:00 PM IST**) are maintained.
- Attendance is recorded via the biometric / Slack-integrated check-in bot (`/check-in` and `/check-out`).

## Section 2: Remote Work & Expense Reimbursement Policy

### 2.1 Remote & Hybrid Work Guidelines
The organization operates under a Remote-First / Flexible-Hybrid model. Employees working remotely are required to maintain a secure, ergonomic workspace with reliable high-speed internet and power backup.

### 2.2 Expense Reimbursement Categories and Limits
Employees can claim reimbursements under the following pre-approved categories via the HRMS portal:
- **High-Speed Broadband Internet Allowance:** Up to **₹1,500 per month** for remote employees. Invoices must be submitted with service address and GST details.
- **Home Office Setup Allowance:** A one-time allowance of up to **₹15,000** for ergonomic chairs, desks, or secondary monitors (applicable after completion of 30 days of service).
- **Learning & Professional Development:** Up to **₹25,000 per financial year** for technical certifications (e.g., Cloud, AI/ML, LangChain, Google Cloud, AWS) and approved conferences.
- **Client Travel & Meals:** Daily allowance of ₹1,200 for domestic outstation meals, and flight bookings must be processed through the corporate travel desk.

### 2.3 Reimbursement Submission & Payout Timelines
- All reimbursement claims must be submitted in the HRMS before the **25th day of the current month**.
- Approved claims are reimbursed in the payroll cycle of the same month. Invoices older than 60 days are not accepted.

## Section 3: Performance Review & Pre-Placement Offer (PPO) Conversion

### 3.1 Evaluation Cycles
- Performance reviews take place semi-annually: Mid-Year Review (June) and Annual Review (December).
- Ratings are based on a 5-point scale: 1 (Unsatisfactory), 2 (Needs Improvement), 3 (Meets Expectations), 4 (Exceeds Expectations), 5 (Outstanding).

### 3.2 Intern Conversion to Full-Time Employee (PPO)
- Interns enrolled in 6-month programs are eligible for Pre-Placement Offer (PPO) evaluation at Month 5.
- Eligibility criteria for full-time conversion:
  1. Performance evaluation rating of **4.0 or higher** across technical milestones and project deliverables.
  2. Completion and successful deployment of assigned AI agent studio projects (e.g., LangGraph, MCP integrations, production guardrails).
  3. Formal written endorsement and recommendation from the reporting Engineering Lead / Manager.
- **Post-Conversion Compensation:** On successful conversion to a permanent Full-Time Employee (FTE), the candidate receives an annual package starting at **₹10,00,000 per annum (₹10 LPA)** base CTC plus benefits, ESOP grants, and annual performance incentives.

## Section 4: Code of Conduct, Grievances, and POSH

### 4.1 Anti-Harassment & POSH Policy
The company maintains zero tolerance for harassment, discrimination, or intimidation. An Internal Complaints Committee (ICC) handles all grievances under the Prevention of Sexual Harassment (POSH) Act with complete confidentiality.
- Contact for ICC complaints: `posh@company.com`

### 4.2 Whistleblower & Grievance Redressal
Employees may raise workplace concerns or report ethical breaches anonymously through the internal grievance portal or directly email `grievance@company.com`. Response SLA is 48 business hours.
